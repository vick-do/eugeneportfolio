( Questions )

Good to know.

How long does a brand identity project take?

Most identity projects take 6 to 10 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly feedback comes back. A clear timeline is agreed at the start.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so I quote after our first call. Identity projects usually start from $1,500. A 50% deposit books your slot.

Do you work with clients outside Ghana?

Yes. I work with clients across Africa, Europe and North America. Calls and presentations happen over video, and time zones are rarely a problem.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included at the Refine stage. Because direction is agreed early, big changes late in the project are rare.

Will I own the final files?

Yes. Once the final payment is made, full ownership of the final brand assets is transferred to you.
