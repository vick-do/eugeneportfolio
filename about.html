Logistics & Supply Chain Management AGI Strategy — BlueDot Impact Creative Associate — MEST Africa

Before I began building visual identities for Africa’s tech ecosystem, I studied Logistics and Supply Chain Management. That background in operations—combined with a recent certification in AGI Strategy from BlueDot Impact—shapes how I design today. I don’t just make things look good; I build scalable visual systems, simplify complex policies, and bridge the gap between technical innovation and public understanding.

Currently the Creative Associate at MEST Africa, I lead creative direction across startup programmes and ecosystem events, while coaching emerging founders on visual storytelling and pitch deck design. From branding youth football clubs to leading cross-functional teams in digital campaigns, my work is driven by curiosity, structure, and the vibrant energy of Accra.
