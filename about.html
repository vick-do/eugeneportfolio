( My Story )

I started out drawing posters for church events and school shows. I’ve been designing for the stage, one way or another, ever since.

Today I work with founders, start-ups and in-house teams to build brand identities that are clear, considered and built to last. I believe good branding isn’t decoration. It’s how a business explains itself when it isn’t in the room. Every project gets the same care, whether it’s a single logo or a full brand system.

When I’m not designing, I’m usually at a gig, collecting old book covers, or trying to find the best waakye in Accra.
