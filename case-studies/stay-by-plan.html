Stay By Plan — The Podcast

Visual identity & creative direction for Stay by Plan, inspired by Accra’s culture, energy, and daily stories.

Client

Stay By Plan

My Role

Rebrand lead & creative direction

Scope

Logo redesign · Visual identity system · Design language · Animated podcast intro · Custom host illustrations · Digital & physical touchpoints

( The Brief )

Stay By Plan had built a loyal audience, but its identity no longer reflected the energy of the conversations it was creating. The goal was to transform the brand into something bold, memorable, and unmistakably rooted in Accra’s culture.

( What I Did )

I led the rebrand from the ground up, redesigning the logo, developing a flexible visual identity system, and establishing a modern design language that could scale across digital and physical touchpoints. To bring the new identity to life, I also created an animated podcast intro featuring custom illustrations of the hosts, the show’s original theme song, and iconic Accra landmarks.

( The Outcome )

The result was a cohesive brand that feels authentic to its audience while giving the podcast a stronger, more distinctive visual presence.
