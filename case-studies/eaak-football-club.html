EAAK Football Club

Visual identity for EAAK Football Club, from logo design to its wider visual system.

Client

EAAK Football Club

My Role

Brand identity designer

Scope

Logo design · Visual identity · Kit & merchandise application · Digital platforms · Community event graphics

( The Brief )

Football has always been more than a sport to me, so partnering with EAAK was a project I connected with on a personal level. As an organization dedicated to empowering young people through football, education, and leadership, EAAK is creating opportunities that extend far beyond the game.

( What I Did )

I designed the organization’s logo and visual identity, creating a brand that captures its ambition, sense of community, and long-term vision. The identity was built to scale across everything from kits and merchandise to digital platforms and community events, giving EAAK a distinctive and memorable presence.

( The Outcome )

Combining my passion for football with my love for branding made this one of the most rewarding identity projects I’ve worked on.
