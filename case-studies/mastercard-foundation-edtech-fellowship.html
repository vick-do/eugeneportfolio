Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship

Created a cohesive visual system across events, campaigns, and digital communications.

Client

Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship

My Role

Creative Associate, MEST Africa

Scope

Launch campaigns · Presentation decks · Email communications · Event branding · Marketing collateral · Pitch Deck Design workshop

( The Brief )

Every stage of the Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship tells a story, from attracting founders with bold campaign visuals to celebrating their progress on Demo Day. My role was to make that journey feel connected through thoughtful, consistent design.

( What I Did )

Working as Creative Associate at MEST Africa, I designed the visual assets that supported the programme throughout its lifecycle, including launch campaigns, presentation decks, email communications, event branding, and marketing collateral. Beyond designing, I facilitated a Pitch Deck Design workshop that helped founders communicate their ideas more effectively to investors.

( The Outcome )

The outcome was a cohesive brand experience that strengthened the fellowship’s presence while supporting the next generation of African EdTech entrepreneurs.
